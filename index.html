About The Boat Inn

Set on Meadow Lane right beside the canal, The Boat Inn is the kind of pub Loughborough was built around — welcoming to all, easy-going, and never trying to be something it isn’t.

We’re a Marston’s pub and a proper community local. Walk in off the towpath with the dog, bring the family for Sunday, or settle in for the quiz — you’ll get the same easy welcome.
